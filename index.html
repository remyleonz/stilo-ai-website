Questions

What exactly do I get?

Qualified meetings with the people who sign, on your calendar, each with a brief on who you are meeting and why they are in the market. In week one you also receive the researched list itself: every company in your territory that fits, with the decision maker's name, direct email and direct line. That list is yours to keep.

Who does the outreach?

A rep assigned to your account and nobody else's, working your list daily across email, phone and text. Your team's involvement begins when a meeting is already on the calendar.

How do you charge?

Two components: a setup fee for the build, and a flat fee for each qualified meeting that shows up. No retainer. The exact figures depend on what a new customer is worth in your business, which is what the first call establishes.

What if a meeting does not meet the standard?

Then it does not count. The five-point definition is part of the agreement. Flag a meeting within five business days, and if it falls short of the standard you are not billed for it and we replace it at no cost.

How soon do meetings land?

Week one is the build: buyer definition, list, research, sequences, your rep. Outreach begins at the end of it, and the first meetings typically land in the second and third week. These are considered sales cycles, so we measure across 60 days.

Do you work with my competitors?

No. One company per industry, per market. Your list and your competitor's list would be the same list, so we could not credibly work both. When a territory is taken, it is taken.
