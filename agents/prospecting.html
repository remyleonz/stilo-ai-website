Everything you'd want to know

Questions, answered straight.

No fine print. Here is exactly what the agent does, how it works, what it costs you, and who you're working with.

What does the B2B Lead Generator actually do?

It fills your calendar with qualified sales meetings. It finds the businesses that match your ideal customer, figures out who the real decision-maker is, and reaches out to them personally across email, LinkedIn, and phone. When someone's interested, it handles the back-and-forth and books the meeting straight onto your calendar. You show up to the call. It does everything before it.

How does it work, step by step?

1. We set your ideal customer profile with you: industry, company size, location, job title, the traits of the accounts you want to close.

2. The agent builds a live list of matching companies and identifies the decision-maker at each one, with verified contact details.

3. It writes outreach that's specific to each prospect, references something real about their business, and sends it across email, LinkedIn, and phone in a sequence over a few days.

4. When a prospect replies, it handles the conversation, answers questions, offers real open times, and books the meeting onto your calendar. You get a prepped calendar invite.

How do you find prospects, and where does the contact data come from?

The agent pulls from the same business databases the big sales teams use, then verifies each contact before anyone gets reached. It matches companies against your ideal customer profile, finds the person who actually signs off on a deal like yours, and confirms their email and role are current. You're not paying for a stale list. You're getting live, checked prospects that fit.

How do you know who my ideal customer is?

We set it with you on a short call at the start. We look at the accounts you've already closed and the ones you wish you had, and turn that into a clear profile: industry, size, region, the title of the person who buys, and the signals that mean a company is a fit. You know your best customer better than anyone. Our job is to turn that into a target the agent can hunt.

Does it personalize each message, or is this spray-and-pray?

Every message is written for that specific prospect. It references their company, their role, something real about what they do, and why you're a fit for them. That's the whole point. Generic blasts get ignored and hurt your sending reputation. Personalized outreach at volume is what actually gets a decision-maker to reply, and it's what this agent is built to do.

What channels does it use?

Email, LinkedIn, and phone. A decision-maker who ignores a cold email might reply on LinkedIn, or pick up a call. Working all three in a coordinated sequence is what lifts reply rates well past any single channel. You can start with email only and add the others once you see it working.

Does it book meetings straight onto my calendar?

Yes. When a prospect is ready to talk, the agent offers your real open times and books the meeting directly onto your calendar with an invite to both sides. You don't chase, you don't coordinate. You open your calendar and there's a qualified meeting on it with a short brief on who you're about to talk to.

Won't cold outreach get flagged as spam?

Not the way we run it. Deliverability is the whole game, so we set up dedicated sending domains, warm them up properly, keep daily volume in a safe range, and watch your sending reputation the entire time. The messages are personalized and relevant, which is exactly what inbox providers reward. Done right, your outreach lands in the inbox instead of the spam folder, and your main domain stays clean.

Is this compliant?

Yes. We run B2B outreach the legitimate way: business contacts, a real reason to reach out, an easy way to opt out, and full respect for anyone who asks not to be contacted again. We handle suppression lists and honor every opt-out automatically. This is professional outreach to businesses, done by the book, not scraped consumer spam.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short call to lock in your ideal customer profile, a sentence or two on your offer, and access to the calendar you want meetings booked on. That's the lift on your end. We handle the sending setup, the domains, the list building, the outreach, and the maintenance. You approve the messaging once, then meetings start showing up.

How long until I start seeing meetings?

We're usually live in under two weeks, most of which is warming up the sending domains so your email actually lands. Once outreach is flowing, the first replies and booked meetings typically come within the first couple of weeks. It builds from there as the agent learns what messaging and targeting convert best for you.

How do I track my pipeline?

You get a live dashboard from day one: how many prospects were contacted, who opened and replied, and every meeting booked. Nothing's a black box. You can see exactly what's working, and the booked meetings land on your own calendar too, so you always know what's coming.

How is this different from hiring an SDR?

A good sales rep costs you a salary plus commission, takes months to ramp, needs managing, takes vacation, and can only work so many accounts a day. The agent works your entire target list across every channel at once, day and night, and never has an off week. You get the output of a full outreach team without the payroll, the ramp time, or the churn when a rep quits.

What businesses is this for?

Any business that sells to other businesses: agencies, consultants, software, professional services, commercial and B2B service providers. If your customer is another company and a real deal is worth going after, this finds those companies, reaches the right person, and books the meeting. If you sell to walk-in consumers, this isn't the right agent, and we'll tell you that on the call.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many prospects you want worked and how many channels you run, so we give you the exact number on the call once we see your target market. What stays the same is how we work: you pay half to start and the other half only once it's built and you're happy with it, plus a flat monthly to keep it running. The point is one closed contract usually covers it many times over.

What if it doesn't work? Can I cancel?

The monthly is not a long lock-in. If it's not putting qualified meetings on your calendar, you're not stuck. We take on the risk up front with the 50% on satisfaction structure because we're confident in what focused outreach to the right people does, but the relationship continues because it works, not because a contract traps you.
