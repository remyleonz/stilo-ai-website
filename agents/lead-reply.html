No fine print. Here is exactly what the agent does, how it works, what it costs you, and who you're working with.

What does the Instant Lead Reply agent actually do?

The second a new lead comes in, from your website form, Google, or Facebook, the agent replies in seconds by text, call, and email. It answers their questions, asks the few things you need to know to qualify them, and books the appointment straight onto your calendar. Instead of a lead sitting in an inbox until someone gets to it, it's already handled before your competitors even see it.

Why does replying first matter so much?

Because the first business to respond usually gets the job. Most people fill out a few forms at once and go with whoever gets back to them first. If you reply in five minutes instead of an hour, you're far more likely to actually reach that lead and close them. After an hour, most of them have already booked with someone else. Speed isn't a nice-to-have here, it's the whole game, and it's the one thing a busy team can't do by hand at 9pm on a Saturday.

How does it work, step by step?

1. We connect your lead sources: your website form, Google, Facebook, wherever new inquiries land.

2. A new lead comes in and the agent replies in seconds by text, and by call and email if you want, in your business's voice.

3. It asks the handful of questions you tell us matter, so you know what the job is before you ever pick up the phone.

4. Once the lead's a fit, it offers real open times and books them straight onto your calendar. Your team just shows up.

What lead sources does it connect to?

The common ones: your website contact form, Google (including Google Local Services / Google ads leads), and Facebook and Instagram lead ads. If a new lead lands somewhere, the agent can pick it up and start the conversation. We map your exact sources with you on the setup call so nothing slips through.

Does it book straight onto my calendar?

Yes. Once a lead qualifies, the agent offers your real open times and drops the confirmed appointment right onto the calendar you already use. No back-and-forth, no double-booking. Your team opens their day and the slots are already filled.

Will leads know they're talking to a bot? Does it sound robotic?

It sounds natural and it speaks in your business's voice, not a stiff script. You approve the wording before anything goes live, and it reads like a fast, sharp reply from your front desk, because that's exactly what it's standing in for. Most leads just feel like they got answered quickly, which is the whole point.

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes. It's fully bilingual and replies to each lead in English or Spanish based on how they reach out. For a lot of Miami businesses that alone wins jobs from leads who'd never respond to an English-only reply.

Does it work after hours and on weekends?

That's where it earns its keep. A huge share of leads come in at night and on weekends, exactly when nobody's watching the inbox. The agent replies in seconds at 11pm on a Sunday the same as it does at 2pm on a Tuesday, so you stop losing the after-hours leads to whoever answers first.

What does it ask to qualify a lead?

Whatever you tell us matters for your business: the type of job, location, budget range, timeline, whether they're a fit for what you offer. You give us the few questions that separate a real job from a tire-kicker, and the agent asks them conversationally up front. You walk into every appointment already knowing what it's about.

Do I keep my current form and phone number?

Yes. It sits on top of what you already have. Your website form, your phone number, your calendar, your lead ads all stay exactly where they are. You don't rip anything out or make customers learn something new. We just plug the agent in behind them so the replies go out instantly.

Is my data secure?

Yes. The agent only touches the lead info it needs to reply and book. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is locked down, and we never sell or share it. For health-related businesses we can sign a BAA. Your data stays yours.

What do you need from me to get started?

Three things: access to your lead sources (form, Google, Facebook), the few questions you want leads qualified on, and the calendar you want appointments booked to. That's the whole lift on your end. We handle the build, the setup, and the maintenance.

How long until it's live?

Typically live in under two weeks. Once it's connected to your lead sources, it starts replying to new inquiries in seconds from day one. You'll see the difference on the very next batch of leads that come in.

How do I know it's actually working?

You get a live dashboard from week one: every lead that came in, how fast it got a reply, who qualified, and who booked. You'll watch response times drop from hours to seconds and see the booked appointments show up on your own calendar. Nothing is a black box.

How is this different from my team replying when they get to it?

Your team is doing the actual work all day, so a lead that comes in at noon might not get a reply until the evening, and the after-hours ones wait until morning. By then the lead has usually gone with whoever answered first. The agent replies in seconds, every time, day or night, and never forgets or gets buried. Your team stops doing frantic follow-up and just runs the appointments that are already on the calendar.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Any business that runs on inbound leads and where speed decides who wins: home services, roofing, HVAC, remodeling, cleaning, legal, med spas, dental, real estate, auto, and more. If you're paying for leads from Google or Facebook, or getting form fills you can't answer fast enough, this makes sure you actually win them.

What does it cost?

It depends on your lead volume and what you want it doing, so we give you the exact number on the call once we see your setup. What stays the same is how we work: you pay half to start and the other half only once it's built and you're happy with it, plus a flat monthly to keep it running. The point is it pays for itself in won jobs, usually in the first month, because it's catching the leads you're already paying for but losing to a slow reply.

What if it doesn't work? Can I cancel?

The monthly is not a long lock-in. If it's not replying fast and putting booked appointments on your calendar, you're not stuck. We take on the risk up front with the 50% on satisfaction structure because we're confident in what speed-to-lead does, but the relationship continues because it works, not because a contract traps you.
