Questions, answered straight.

No fine print. Here is exactly what the agent does, how it works, what it costs you, and who you're working with.

What does the AI Sales Agent actually do?

It listens to every sales call your team makes, scores each one, and tells each rep exactly what to do differently next time. Then it runs the follow-ups: the emails and texts that keep deals moving after the call ends. Think of it as a sales manager who sits in on every conversation, never gets tired, and coaches everyone at once. Your best rep already knows the right moves. This gets the whole team making them.

Who is this for?

Teams of three or more reps. Once you have a few people selling, the gap between your best closer and everyone else is where the money leaks. One person can't ride along on every call and coach every rep after every one. This does. If you're a solo seller it's overkill, but the moment you're managing a team, this is the tool that lifts the bottom half up to the top.

How does it work, step by step?

1. We connect your call recording and your CRM. Whatever your team already uses to make and track calls.

2. After each call, the agent scores it against what actually closes deals in your business and writes up a short breakdown for that rep.

3. The rep gets specific coaching: what they did well, where they lost the room, and the exact line to use next time.

4. The agent runs the follow-ups automatically, so no deal dies because someone forgot to send the recap or the next-step email.

How does it analyze calls?

It works off your call recordings and your CRM. It transcribes each call, then reads it against the moments that matter in a sale: how the rep opened, whether they dug into the real problem, how they handled objections, whether they asked for the close. It cross-references the outcome in your CRM so it learns what your winning calls sound like versus the ones that stall. Nothing extra for your reps to log. It reads what's already there.

What kind of coaching does it actually give?

Specific, per rep, tied to real moments in their calls. Not "improve your objection handling." More like: "At 6:12 the prospect said the price felt high and you dropped to a discount. Here's what your top closer says instead when they hear that." Every rep gets their own feedback based on their own calls, so the person who rushes the close and the person who never asks for it get different notes. It's the coaching a great manager would give if they had time to review every single call.

Does it also run outbound and follow-ups?

Yes. Coaching only helps if the deal is still alive, and most deals die in the gap between calls. The agent handles the follow-up sequence: the recap after the call, the next-step email, the nudge when a prospect goes quiet. It keeps every deal moving so your reps spend their time selling, not chasing people down or remembering who they were supposed to email back.

Will my reps resent it? Will they feel watched?

The reps who use it stop dreading it fast, because it's on their side. This isn't a manager scrolling their calls looking for mistakes to bring up in a one-on-one. It's private, useful feedback that helps them hit quota and make more money. The framing matters: it's a coach in their corner, not a camera over their shoulder. Good reps want to get better, and this hands them exactly what to fix without waiting for a monthly review. The ones who close more take home more. That's the whole point.

Is call data private and secure?

Yes. Call recordings and CRM data are encrypted in transit and at rest, access is locked down to your team, and we never sell or share any of it. Coaching feedback goes to the rep and whoever you decide should see it, nobody else. Your data stays yours, and we can sign whatever agreement your business needs before a single call is processed.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to your call recordings, your CRM, and a quick session where you tell us what a good call looks like in your business. That's the lift on your end. We handle the build, the setup, and the tuning, and we calibrate the scoring to how you actually sell, not some generic playbook. Then it starts working from your team's very next calls.

How long until I see an impact?

Typically live in under two weeks. Reps start getting coaching on their next calls, and the follow-up automation kicks in right away, so no deal falls through the cracks from day one. The bigger lift, the whole team's close rate climbing, usually shows up over the first month or two as the coaching compounds and your weaker reps start sounding like your strong ones.

How do I measure it?

You get a dashboard with the numbers that matter: close rate by rep, talk-to-listen ratio, how well each person handles objections and asks for the close, and a leaderboard so you can see who's improving and who needs a hand. You'll watch the gap between your top and bottom reps shrink. And you'll see it in the one number that counts, more deals closed from the same pipeline.

How is this different from Gong, or a manager coaching reps by hand?

Gong records and analyzes calls, but you still need a person to sit down, watch them, and coach each rep. That person is your bottleneck. A manager can review maybe a handful of calls a week, so most calls never get looked at and most reps get feedback once a month if they're lucky. This scores every call and delivers the coaching automatically, per rep, plus it runs the follow-ups. You get the coaching output of a full-time sales manager riding along on every single call, without the salary or the time.

What kinds of teams is this for?

Any team that sells over the phone or on calls: insurance, solar, home services, roofing, mortgage, real estate, agencies, B2B, and more. If your revenue depends on reps having good conversations and closing them, this makes every one of those conversations sharper. The industry matters less than the setup: a few reps, real deals on the line, and a top performer whose habits you'd love to clone across the team.

The relationship continues because it works, not because a contract traps you.
