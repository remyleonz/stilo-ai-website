Questions, answered straight.

No fine print. Here is exactly what the agent does, how it works, what it costs you, and who you're working with.

What does the Reactivation agent actually do?

It takes your list of past customers, the ones who came in once or twice and then went quiet, and it personally reaches back out to each of them by text, call, and email to get them booked back in. It handles the whole conversation: answering questions, offering times, and putting the appointment on your calendar. You wake up to filled slots from people you'd already written off.

How does it work, step by step?

1. We connect your past-customer list (a simple export from whatever software you use, or a spreadsheet).

2. The agent flags who's gone quiet and sorts them by value, so your best opportunities get worked first.

3. You approve the message once, add an offer if you want, and it goes out across text, call, and email over a few days.

4. When someone replies, the agent answers their questions, offers real open times, and books them straight onto your calendar. It stops the moment they book.

Where does my customer list come from? Do I have to build it?

You already have it. It's the customers in your booking software, your CRM, your point-of-sale, or even a spreadsheet of names and numbers. Almost every system exports a customer list in a couple of clicks, and we handle the setup with you on a short call. You don't build anything.

Will my customers know they're talking to a bot? Does it sound robotic?

It sounds natural and it speaks in your business's voice, not a stiff script. You approve the wording before anything goes out, and you can start with text only if you want a softer touch. The goal is that it reads like a thoughtful message from your front desk, because that's exactly what it's standing in for.

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes. It's fully bilingual and will talk to each customer in English or Spanish. For a lot of Miami businesses that alone recovers customers who never respond to English-only outreach.

Is my customer data safe?

Yes. The agent only touches the contact info it needs to reach your customers. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is locked down, and we never sell or share it. For health-related businesses we can sign a BAA. Your data stays yours.

Do I have to switch the software I already use?

No. We sit on top of what you have. Your booking system, your calendar, and your phone stay exactly where they are and stay the source of truth. You hand us the list, the agent works it, and confirmed bookings show up on your calendar for your team to run with.

What do you need from me to get started?

Three things: your past-customer list, a few minutes to approve the message, and access to the calendar you want bookings to land on. That's the whole lift on your end. We handle the build, the setup, and the maintenance.

How long until it's live and I start seeing bookings?

Typically live in under two weeks. Because it's working a warm list of people who already know you, the first bookings usually come in the first few days of the campaign going out, not months later.

How do I know it's actually working?

You get a live dashboard from week one: who was contacted, who replied, who booked, and the revenue recovered. Nothing is a black box. You'll see the filled appointments on your own calendar too.

How is this different from hiring someone to make the calls, or doing it myself?

A person can work maybe a few dozen names a day between everything else on their plate, and it's the first thing that gets dropped when the day gets busy. The agent works your entire list at once, every channel, nights and weekends, and never gets tired or skips it. You get the output of a full outreach team without the payroll or the management.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Any business with repeat customers and a list of people who've lapsed: dental and medical practices, med spas and salons, home services, cleaning, auto, fitness, and more. If people are supposed to come back on a cycle and some of them quietly stopped, this recovers them.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of your list and what you want it doing, so we give you the exact number on the call once we see your setup. What stays the same is how we work: you pay half to start and the other half only once it's built and you're happy with it, plus a flat monthly to keep it running. The point is it pays for itself in recovered bookings, usually in the first month.

What if it doesn't work? Can I cancel?

The monthly is not a long lock-in. If it's not putting bookings on your calendar, you're not stuck. We take on the risk up front with the 50% on satisfaction structure because we're confident in what a warm list will do, but the relationship continues because it works, not because a contract traps you.
