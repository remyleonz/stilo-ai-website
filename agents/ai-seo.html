What does the AI SEO agent actually do?

It gets your business named when people ask an AI tool or a search engine for the best option near them. When someone types "best dentist near me" into ChatGPT, "top med spa in Miami" into Perplexity, or a plain question into Google, the answer they read back should be you. We rebuild how your business shows up across the web so the AI models and search engines pull your name, your reviews, and your details into that answer. You go from invisible in the place people now shop to the one they get pointed to first.

What is AI SEO (or GEO), and how is it different from regular SEO?

Regular SEO is about ranking on a page of blue links. AI SEO, sometimes called GEO for generative engine optimization, is about getting cited inside the answer the AI writes. Nobody scrolls ten links anymore. They ask ChatGPT or read Google's AI summary at the top and take the names it gives them. Those two jobs overlap, but they're not the same. We do the search fundamentals that still matter and the newer work that gets you quoted by the models, because that's where your customers actually are now.

How does it work, step by step?

1. We check where you stand today: we ask the AI tools and search engines the exact questions your customers ask and see whether you come up at all, and where your competitors beat you.

2. We fix the foundation the models trust: your business listings, your reviews, the way your site describes what you do and where, and the details that get cross-checked before you're recommended.

3. We build the content and structured data the AI tools pull from, so when someone asks about your category, your name is the clean, obvious answer to serve.

4. We re-test the same questions and show you the before and after: where you now surface across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google, and search.

How do you actually get me into ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews?

These tools don't take payment for placement. They read the open web and pull the businesses that look the most trustworthy and the most clearly relevant to the question. So we make you exactly that. We clean up and align your listings so your name, category, and location match everywhere. We shape your reviews and your site content around the real questions people ask. And we add the structured signals the models look for when they decide who to name. It's not a trick or a paid slot. It's making your business the answer the model would pick anyway.

How fast do results show up?

The foundation work is usually live in under two weeks. The listing and search side tends to move first. The AI tools refresh how they read the web on their own schedule, so being named in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers builds over the following weeks as they pick up the changes. We re-test throughout so you can watch your visibility climb, not just take our word for it.

How do you measure whether it's working?

We track visibility across the actual tools. We ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI answers, and regular search the same set of buyer questions on a schedule, and we log whether you get named, how you're described, and where you sit against competitors. You get a clear before-and-after, not a vague promise. If you weren't coming up before and now you are, that's the whole point, and you'll see it plainly.

Do I need a new website first?

No. We work with the site you have. Most of what moves the needle here lives beyond your homepage anyway: your listings, your reviews, the structured details across the web, and specific content the models read. If your site is genuinely holding you back we'll tell you straight, but you don't need to rebuild anything to start.

Will this keep working as the AI models change?

The tools keep shifting, but what they reward doesn't: a business that's clearly described, consistently listed, well reviewed, and obviously relevant to the question. We build on those durable signals, not on some loophole in this month's model. So the work holds up as the tools evolve. If you ever want a fresh tune-up down the road as the space matures, we're here, but you're not tied to a monthly for it.
