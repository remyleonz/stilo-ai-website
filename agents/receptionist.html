No fine print. Here is exactly what the agent does, how it works, what it costs you, and who you're working with.

What does the Receptionist agent actually do?

It answers your phone. Every call you can't get to, day or night, gets picked up on the first couple of rings by a voice that sounds like your front desk. It greets the caller, answers the usual questions about hours, pricing, and services, figures out what the caller needs, and books the appointment straight onto your calendar. The calls you used to lose to voicemail turn into booked jobs.

How does it work, step by step?

1. We forward your business number to the agent, either for every call or only the ones your team can't pick up. Your number doesn't change.

2. When a call comes in, the agent answers in your business's voice, in English or Spanish, and handles the conversation.

3. It asks the right questions to qualify the job, what the caller needs, when, and whether they're a fit, then offers real open times.

4. It books the appointment onto your calendar and logs the call. Anything it can't handle gets passed to you with a summary so nothing slips.

Does it replace my front desk, or just back it up?

Your call. Most businesses have it catch the overflow: the calls that come in while the phone's already busy, during lunch, after hours, or on weekends. Your team still takes the calls they can. Some smaller shops run it as the whole front line. Either way you stop losing the calls nobody was around to answer.

How does it handle after-hours and emergencies?

It never clocks out. Nights, weekends, and holidays, it answers the same way it does at noon on a Tuesday. For true emergencies you tell us the rules up front: what counts as urgent, and who to reach. When one of those comes in, it can take a message, text or call you right away, or route the caller to your on-call line. You set the line between "book it for tomorrow" and "wake someone up."

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes. It's fully bilingual and handles the whole call in English or Spanish, switching to whatever the caller speaks. For a lot of Miami businesses that alone captures customers who'd hang up on an English-only line.

Will callers know it's AI? Does it sound robotic?

It sounds like a real person: natural voice, natural pacing, and it can handle interruptions and follow-up questions without falling apart. It greets people in your business's name and tone, not a canned script. Most callers just think they reached a helpful front desk, which is the point.

What happens on a call it can't handle?

It doesn't guess or make something up. If a caller needs something outside what it's set up to do, it takes down the details, tells them someone will follow up, and hands it straight to you with a short summary by text or email. You decide up front which situations get escalated live versus logged for later. Nothing gets dropped on the floor.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. You keep the number your customers already know. We set up a simple forward so calls ring to the agent, either all of them or only the ones you miss, and nothing about your line changes. No new number to advertise, no porting headache, no switching carriers.

Is my data secure?

Yes. The agent only handles the call details and contact info it needs to book the job. Everything's encrypted in transit and at rest, access is locked down, and we never sell or share it. For health-related businesses we can sign a BAA. Your data and your customers stay yours.

What do you need from me to get started?

Not much: the number you want it to answer, a rundown of how you want calls handled (hours, pricing, common questions, what to book), and access to the calendar you want appointments to land on. We build it around your business, you review it, and we handle the setup and the upkeep from there.

How long until it's live?

Typically live in under two weeks. We build it to your business, you listen to it handle a few test calls, and once it sounds right we point your line at it. From that point on, the calls you used to miss get answered.

How do I know it's working?

You get a live dashboard: every call answered, what the caller wanted, what got booked, and the recordings and summaries to go with it. Nothing is a black box. You'll see the appointments show up on your own calendar too, so the results are right in front of you.

How is this different from a call center or an answering service?

An answering service usually just takes a message and hands you a callback list, which means you're still doing the real work later. This actually handles the call: it answers questions, qualifies the job, and books it on the spot, in your voice, at any hour. No hold music, no accent barrier, no per-minute meter running, and no team of strangers reading your callers a script that isn't yours.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Any business where a missed call is a lost customer: dental and medical practices, med spas and salons, home services like HVAC, plumbing, and electrical, cleaning, auto, law firms, and more. If people call to book you and you can't always pick up, this catches the ones you'd otherwise lose.

What does it cost?

It depends on your call volume and how you want it handling things, so we give you the exact number on the call once we see your setup. What stays the same is how we work: you pay half to start and the other half only once it's built and you're happy with it, plus a flat monthly to keep it running. The point is it pays for itself in booked jobs, usually from the calls you were already missing.

What if it doesn't work? Can I cancel?

The monthly is not a long lock-in. If it's not answering your calls and putting bookings on your calendar, you're not stuck. We take on the risk up front with the 50% on satisfaction structure because we're confident in what answering every call will do, but the relationship continues because it works, not because a contract traps you.
