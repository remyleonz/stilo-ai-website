Questions

How long does it take?

Most sites go live in under two weeks. We move fast because we handle the build, the copy, and the setup for you. You review it, we adjust, and it ships. No months-long web project that drags on and never launches.

Do I own the site? What happens if I ever leave?

The domain is yours and stays yours, always. We build and host the site for you, and while we work together we keep it running, fast, and updated. If you ever decide to part ways, you keep your domain and your content, and we hand off cleanly. You're never held hostage by us.

Is my data secure?

Yes. The site runs on secure hosting with HTTPS, and anything a visitor submits, a booking, a chat, a form, is encrypted in transit and handled carefully. We don't sell or share your data or your customers' data. Your information stays yours.

How do I know it's working?

You get analytics from day one: how many people visit, where they come from, and how many book. The booking number is the one that matters, and you'll see it clearly. Bookings from the site also show up right on your calendar, so it's never a guessing game whether the site is earning its keep.

How is this different from Wix or Squarespace, or hiring a freelancer?

A DIY builder hands you the tools and leaves the work, the writing, and the maintenance to you, and the result usually looks like a template. A freelancer builds it, invoices you, and disappears, so when something breaks or needs updating you're on your own. We build it, write it, host it, keep it fast, and update it whenever you ask, and it's designed to book customers, not just to exist. You get a finished site plus a partner on the hook for it.

Who is STILO? Who am I actually working with?

STILO AI Partners is a Miami-based team that builds custom AI agents and websites for local businesses. You work directly with a partner, a real person you can call, not a support queue. We build it, we run it, and we're on the hook for the results. You can reach us any time at remyleon@stiloaipartners.com or (786) 837-6639.
