Everything you'd want to know

Questions, answered straight.

No fine print. Here is exactly what you get, how it works, what it costs you, and who you're working with.

What do I actually get?

A modern, fast website built to turn visitors into booked customers. It comes with an online booking widget, live chat, and SEO built in from day one, all designed around your business and wired to the calendar and phone you already use. Not a template you have to figure out. A finished site that's ready to bring you appointments.

Do you rebuild my existing site or start from scratch?

Whichever gets you a better result. If you have a site, we usually rebuild it so it looks current and converts, and you keep your domain and your Google history so you don't lose any ranking you've earned. If you don't have one, or the old one isn't worth saving, we build fresh. Either way you end up on a clean, fast site that's yours.

How long does it take?

Most sites go live in under two weeks. We move fast because we handle the build, the copy, and the setup for you. You review it, we adjust, and it ships. No months-long web project that drags on and never launches.

Do I own the site? What happens if I ever leave?

The domain is yours and stays yours, always. We build and host the site for you, and while we work together we keep it running, fast, and updated. If you ever decide to part ways, you keep your domain and your content, and we hand off cleanly. You're never held hostage by us.

What are the conversion features you keep mentioning?

Booking widget: visitors pick a time and book right on the page, and it lands on your calendar.

Live chat: questions get answered on the spot instead of a visitor bouncing to a competitor.

Mobile-perfect: it looks sharp and loads fast on a phone, which is where most of your traffic actually is.

These are the pieces that turn a nice-looking page into one that puts appointments on your calendar.

Is SEO included?

Yes. The site is built so Google can read it, rank it, and show it to people searching for what you do. Clean structure, fast load times, proper titles and tags, and location signals all baked in from the start. You're not paying extra later to make a slow site findable.

Do I have to write the content?

No. We write it. We pull what we need from your current site, a short call, and what we can see about your business, then we draft the copy and lay it out. You review and tell us what to tweak. If you want to hand us specific wording or photos you love, great, but the writing isn't on your plate.

What does the monthly cover?

Hosting, updates, and keeping the site fast and secure. That means it stays online, it stays quick, and when you want to change hours, add a service, swap a photo, or update an offer, you ask and we handle it. No plugins to babysit, no surprise breakage, no separate hosting bill to chase. We give you the exact monthly number on the call.

Does it connect to my calendar and booking?

Yes. The booking widget ties into the calendar or booking system you already run, so a booking on the site shows up where your team already looks. We handle the setup with you. Your calendar stays the source of truth, the site just feeds it.

Is my data secure?

Yes. The site runs on secure hosting with HTTPS, and anything a visitor submits, a booking, a chat, a form, is encrypted in transit and handled carefully. We don't sell or share your data or your customers' data. Your information stays yours.

What do you need from me to start?

Not much. Access to your domain, a short call to understand your business and your offers, and the calendar you want bookings to land on. If you have photos or brand colors you want used, send them. We take it from there and handle the build, the copy, and the launch.

How do I know it's working?

You get analytics from day one: how many people visit, where they come from, and how many book. The booking number is the one that matters, and you'll see it clearly. Bookings from the site also show up right on your calendar, so it's never a guessing game whether the site is earning its keep.

How is this different from Wix or Squarespace, or hiring a freelancer?

A DIY builder hands you the tools and leaves the work, the writing, and the maintenance to you, and the result usually looks like a template. A freelancer builds it, invoices you, and disappears, so when something breaks or needs updating you're on your own. We build it, write it, host it, keep it fast, and update it whenever you ask, and it's designed to book customers, not just to exist. You get a finished site plus a partner on the hook for it.

What businesses is this for?

Any local business that gets customers through appointments or inquiries and has a dated, slow, or converting-poorly site, or none at all: dental and medical practices, med spas and salons, home services, cleaning, auto, fitness, law and accounting, and more. If people find you online and you want more of them to actually book, this is for you.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of the site and what you want it doing, so we give you the exact number on the call once we see your current setup. What stays the same is how we work: you pay half to start and the other half only once it's built and you're happy with it, plus a flat monthly to host and maintain it. The point is a site that books customers pays for itself, usually fast.
